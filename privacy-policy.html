Privacy Policy for GamesPlat.com

Last updated: October 2026

At GamesPlat (accessible from https://gamesplat.com), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by GamesPlat and how we use it.

Log Files

GamesPlat follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected includes IP addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, and referring/exit pages.

Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, GamesPlat uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited.

Google DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is one of a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

CCPA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the CCPA, California consumers have the right to request disclosure, deletion, and that a business not sell their personal data.

GDPR Data Protection Rights

Every user is entitled to data access, rectification, erasure, and restriction of processing under applicable regulations.
