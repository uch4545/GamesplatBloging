Every elementary school teacher dreads the words: *"Attention staff, today will be indoor recess due to inclement weather."* 

When students miss out on playground fresh air, classroom energy quickly boils over. But indoor recess does not have to mean unruly chaos or plopping kids down in front of a screen for 30 minutes. Here are 15 engaging, low-mess games teachers swear by.

## Top Indoor Recess Games List
1. **Four Corners** — Active elimination without the running
2. **Silent Ball** — Absolute focus and zero speaking
3. **Heads Up, Seven Up** — The golden nostalgic classic
4. **Whiteboard Pictionary Relay** — Visual teamwork
5. **Freeze Dance (Floor is Lava)** — Kinetic energy buster

---

## 1. Silent Ball (The Ultimate Quiet Game)

- **Ages:** Grades 1-6
- **Setup Time:** 30 seconds
- **Supplies Needed:** 1 soft foam dodgeball or squishy stress ball.

### Rules:
1. Students may sit on top of their desks or stand around the classroom perimeter.
2. The rules are absolute: **no talking, no laughing, no bad throws, and no drops**.
3. Students gently toss the soft ball to any classmate across the room.
4. A student sits down in their chair if:
   - They drop a catchable ball.
   - They throw an uncatchable pass.
   - They make any sound or whisper.
5. The last student remaining silent and standing wins the round!

> **💡 Teacher Tip:** If students get too good at the game, add a 3-second holding timer or introduce a second ball into rotation.

---

## 2. Four Corners

- **Ages:** Grades K-5
- **Setup Time:** 1 minute
- **Supplies Needed:** Numbers 1 to 4 taped in classroom corners.

### Rules:
1. Label the four corners of your room with numbers 1, 2, 3, and 4.
2. Select one student to be "It". That student stands in the center with their eyes closed and counts backward out loud from 10 to 0.
3. While counting, all other students quietly walk to any of the four corners.
4. When the counter reaches 0, they call out one number (e.g., *"Corner 3!"*).
5. All students standing in that designated corner must sit down at their desks.
6. Repeat until only one or two winners remain!
