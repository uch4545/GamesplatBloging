If you are looking for an energetic card game that gets everyone leaning forward on the edge of their chairs, **Spoons** is an all-time classic. It combines the matching mechanics of Uno with the lightning-fast reflexes of musical chairs.

## The Objective of Spoons
The primary goal is to be the first player to collect **four cards of the same rank** (e.g., four 7s, four Queens, or four Aces) and grab a spoon from the center of the table. Once one spoon is grabbed, all other players must scramble to grab one of the remaining spoons. The player left empty-handed receives a letter!

---

## Game Setup

1. **Count the Players:** Count the total number of participants around the table.
2. **Count the Spoons:** Place spoons in the center of the table within easy reach of all players. You need **one fewer spoon than the number of players** (e.g., 6 players = 5 spoons).
3. **Prepare the Deck:** Use a standard 52-card deck. Shuffle thoroughly.
4. **Deal the Cards:** Deal **4 cards face down** to each player.
5. Place the remainder of the deck face down next to the dealer as the draw pile.

---

## Step-by-Step Rules of Play

### 1. The Continuous Pass
Spoons is not played in slow turns. Passing happens continuously and as quickly as possible:
- The dealer picks up the top card from the draw pile, inspects it, and decides whether to keep it or pass it.
- The dealer discards one card face-down to their left.
- The next player picks up that card, decides whether to swap it into their hand or pass it to their left.
- The final player places unwanted cards into a discard pile next to them.

### 2. Matching Four of a Kind
You are striving to hold four cards of identical rank. Never keep more than 4 cards in your active hand at any moment.

### 3. Grabbing a Spoon
The instant a player collects four matching cards, they grab a spoon. There are two strategic ways to do this:
- **The Loud Smash:** Slam down your hand, yell "SPOONS!", and snatch the spoon. This causes immediate, hilarious panic.
- **The Stealth Grab:** Slide a spoon into your lap quietly without saying a word while continuing to pretend to pass cards. See how long it takes the rest of the table to notice!

### 4. The Scramble
The split second any player grabs a spoon, **every other player can grab a spoon**, even if they do not have four of a kind! The player who fails to grab a spoon is left out.

---

## Letters and Elimination (Winning the Game)

Each round, the player left without a spoon earns a letter in the word **S-P-O-O-N**:
1. 1st loss: **S**
2. 2nd loss: **S-P**
3. 3rd loss: **S-P-O**
4. 4th loss: **S-P-O-O**
5. 5th loss: **S-P-O-O-N** (Eliminated from the game)

When a player is eliminated, remove one spoon from the center and continue playing until only one grand champion remains!

> **💡 House Rule Variation (Forks):** If you are playing in a vacation cabin or college dorm without spoons, replace them with forks, wrapped candy bars, or guitar picks!
